Aged Cheddar & Black Pepper

Bold. Sharp. And decadently savoury.

A more indulgent take on the crunch.

LYLO combines the light texture of popped lotus seeds with the rich character of aged cheddar and a sharp hit of black pepper.

The Flavour

Where rich meets sharp.

Rich, savoury cheddar notes meet the warm character of black pepper, balanced by LYLO's signature light and crunchy texture.

Tasting notes

  • First impression: Rich and savoury
  • Texture: Light, crisp and crunchy
  • Flavour: Aged cheddar with a pronounced savoury character
  • Finish: Warm, peppery and lingering
Pair It With
  • Toasted sandwiches
  • Tomato-based dishes
  • Crisp salads
  • Fresh apple or pear
  • Your favourite cold drink

Why Aged Cheddar & Black Pepper?

Smart Food. Bold flavour.
