Frequently
Asked Questions

LYLO is a crunchy smartfood snack made from popped water lily seeds, commonly known as lotus seeds. Our ingredients are sourced from the wetlands of the Himalayan foothills and LYLO is created in Europe for modern snacking.

LYLO is made from water lily seeds (Euryale ferox), commonly known as lotus seeds. When popped, they become light and crunchy, creating LYLO's distinctive texture.

LYLO combines a naturally simple ingredient with contemporary flavours and European refinement to create a modern take on lotus seed snacking.

The current LYLO range includes Sea Salt, Aged Cheddar & Black Pepper, White Truffle & Sea Salt, and Strawberry.

LYLO starts with naturally sourced popped lotus seeds, with each flavour finished with its respective seasoning or flavouring. For the full ingredient list, please check the individual product page or product packaging.

Yes. LYLO is gluten-free.

LYLO's lotus-seed base is plant-based. However, vegan status may vary by flavour depending on the final formulation. Check the individual product packaging for the confirmed dietary status.

Allergen information varies by flavour. Some LYLO flavours contain milk or milk-derived ingredients. Please always check the allergen information on the individual product packaging before consuming.

Yes. All four current LYLO flavours are labelled High in Fibre.

Each LYLO pack contains between 97 and 126 kcal, depending on the flavour.
  • Sea Salt — 97 kcal per pack
  • White Truffle & Sea Salt — 109 kcal per pack
  • Aged Cheddar & Black Pepper — 109 kcal per pack
  • Strawberry — 126 kcal per pack

Store LYLO in a cool, dry place. Once opened, keep the pack tightly closed to retain its crunch.
