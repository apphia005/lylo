Sea Salt

Clean. Classic. Crunchy.

Nothing complicated. Just the natural crunch of popped lotus seeds with a clean touch of sea salt.

LYLO Sea Salt brings together a naturally simple ingredient and a timeless flavour profile, creating an effortless snack for everyday moments.

The Flavour

Simple by design. Full of crunch.

Sea Salt is LYLO at its most straightforward. The light, airy texture of precision-popped lotus seeds meets a delicate seasoning of sea salt, allowing the ingredient and its satisfying crunch to take centre stage.

Tasting notes

  • First impression: Clean and lightly savoury
  • Texture: Light, crisp and crunchy
  • Flavour: Delicate sea salt with a naturally subtle base
  • Finish: Clean, savoury and moreish
Pair It With

  • Charcuterie boards
  • Soups & light lunches
