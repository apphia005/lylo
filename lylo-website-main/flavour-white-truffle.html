White Truffle & Sea Salt

Delicate. Savoury. Elevated.

A refined take on the everyday crunch.

Earthy truffle notes meet delicate sea salt and the light texture of popped lotus seeds for a different take on everyday snacking.

The Flavour

Where earthy meets delicate.

The contrast of earthy truffle and delicate sea salt, finished with a light, crisp crunch.
